## 4.3 Industry-wide issues from the supplied notes

- Opaque earnings and distance calculations.
- Account deactivation with unclear reasons or slow appeals.
- Delivery-time pressure and rider safety concerns.
- Changing gig-worker social security and compliance requirements.
- Account sharing and fake identities.
- Regional-language and inclusion gaps.
- Difficulty resolving route, fare, payout, and non-delivery disputes.

## 4.4 Differentiation opportunities for this product

1. Itemized earnings for every job, including the calculation basis and deductions.
2. Clear account restriction reason, evidence summary, human appeal, and response timeline.
3. Document eligibility dashboard with expiry and next action.
4. Safety-first dispatch and UI without incentives that encourage rushing.
5. Predictable payout status, failure reasons, and reconciliation.
6. Regional language support throughout onboarding, jobs, earnings, and support.
7. Identity re-checks with retry and manual review to reduce wrongful lockouts.
8. Road, unsafe-area, wrong-pin, and inaccessible-pickup reporting.
9. Evidence-based dispute handling for customers, merchants, and partners.
